What Circadian makes

Natural pillows and duvets with a fill you adjust to your own sleep. Every pillow ships overstuffed; you remove fill through the side zipper until the loft is right, so one pillow fits how you actually sleep. The fills:

  • Organic cotton and wool — the two lines carrying full Global Organic Textile Standard (GOTS) certification.
  • Kapok — a silky, plant-down fiber from the kapok tree.
  • Buckwheat hulls — firm, breathable, and quiet, with airflow between the hulls.
  • Latex — tree-tapped, resilient, for sleepers who want steady support.
  • Buckwool — a buckwheat-and-wool hybrid.

Duvets round out the bedding line. Everything is made by hand in a New Jersey workshop.
